Market lifecycle

From question to payout

  1. CreateQuestion, 2–8 outcomes, close and resolution times, collateral, starting liquidity and odds
  2. TradeAMM buy and sell, P2P orders, add or remove liquidity, mint or merge sets
  3. CloseAMM trading stops at the close time, to protect LPs. P2P orders and minting sets stay open until resolution
  4. ResolveBy the arbitrator, a reporter vote, or a timeout
  5. Redeem1 collateral per winning share. LPs withdraw what they are owed
Three ways to resolve
Named arbitrator

Fixed when the market is created. Reports once, from the resolution time and within 7 days. Can also declare the market invalid.

e.g. Dave reports “Yes”
Reputation-weighted vote

Registered reporters vote, weighted by reputation, with a 50% quorum. Agreeing with the consensus earns +10; disagreeing costs 20%.

ReputationOracle is the arbitrator
Timeout

No report within 7 days: anyone can resolve the market as invalid, and every share pays 1/N of a unit of collateral.

No funds stay locked
